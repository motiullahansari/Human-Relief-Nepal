Eye Camps

Cataract is one of the most common causes of blindness, and a short operation can bring sight back. This project brings eye check-ups to communities and helps patients reach surgery.

Support this projectAsk on WhatsApp
◉

Eye check-ups, glasses and cataract surgery through partner eye hospitals, so people can see again.

What your support can cover

Needs change through the year. Ask our team what is needed most right now.

Eye check-ups

Screening in villages where eye clinics are far away.

Cataract surgery

Surgery at a partner eye hospital for patients who need it.

Glasses

Glasses for children and adults who cannot afford them.

Travel and follow-up

Transport to the hospital and a check after the operation.

iOperations are done by qualified eye surgeons at partner hospitals, not by our team.

How to give to this project

  1. 1Choose the amount you would like to give. You can ask us about current needs first.
  2. 2Transfer to one of our official accounts, with your name and the purpose in the payment reference.
  3. 3Send us a message after the transfer so we can confirm it and record it for this purpose.
  4. 4Follow our field updates on the home page, or ask us for a report.
