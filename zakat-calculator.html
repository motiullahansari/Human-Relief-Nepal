How this estimate works

Net wealth = zakatable assets minus eligible deductions (minimum zero). If it meets your selected nisab and your zakat date has arrived, the estimate is 2.5% of the full net amount, not just the amount above nisab.

This calculator offers 612.36 g of pure silver or 87.48 g of pure gold. Choose the basis you follow, or enter your own nisab for a different standard.

This is a general estimate for zakat on wealth. Jewellery, investments, pensions, debts and other assets may need specific scholarly guidance.
